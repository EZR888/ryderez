Want to read my memoir on your e-reader? Choose the device you have below. You don't need to buy anything.

I have a Kobo

This is the easiest one. If you're viewing this page on your Kobo, just tap the button below.

Download The Escape

After it downloads, the book should appear in your Kobo library.

I have a Kindle

Kindle uses Amazon's free Send to Kindle service. It sounds more complicated than it is.

  1. First, download the book:

    Download The Escape
  2. Then open Amazon's Send to Kindle page:

    Open Send to Kindle
  3. Sign in to Amazon if asked.
  4. Select the TheEscape.epub file you just downloaded.
  5. Amazon will send it to your Kindle.

You do not need to convert the file yourself. Amazon does that automatically.

I don't have an e-reader

That's fine too. You can download the EPUB file and read it with many book-reading apps on a phone, tablet, Mac, or PC.
